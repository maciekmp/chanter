    How to play

    Sing

    • Hold a piano key, or a computer key A W S … '.
    • Press & drag the pad: left–right is pitch, up–down is vowel.
    • While a key is held, the pad (or ↑ ↓, 1–5, or dragging up/down on the key) changes only the vowel.
    • Z / X shift the keyboard an octave.

    Shape

    • Glide: how slowly the voice slides between notes.
    • Echo Mix: dry voice ↔ tempo-synced echo.
    • Voice: baritone → soprano vocal character.

    Loop

    • Space plays the selected track after a one-bar count-in.
    • R records a 2/4/8-bar melody loop in time with the track; it replays every cycle. Playing live takes over from the replay.
